sweaten their mouth with sweets.

2) College life

A simple girl, Unknown place, Unknown people and unwanted course.

This story is about the entire college life of a girl named seema. I hope you all
will enjoy this story. Let's begin.....

There was a girl named seema. she was living in surat city with her family and
currently completed her twelve standard with science stream. She wanted to become a doctor and so she was preparing
for the entrance exam. She and her family was living a happy life.

All days are not same so after some days 12th result was released and seema does
not got satisfied result. Even it was very less. She started living alone we can say that she quarantine herself.
Even she was not talking to her freinds and continuously preparing for the entrance exams.

She gave many entrance exams but she was not qualified and in which she was qualified
she does not get the satisfied course. So she was thinking what to do next.

At that time her father investigated about some courses and some colleges.

One of the college was nearest to her home, so one day she visted that college with
her brother to take the information about the course.

That college was not having the attractive or good infrastructure .And most of the
people in that college was using their local language for communication. So seema does not liked that college.

In that college she got admission in the bcom course. After that curriculum year
begins and she started going to the college. When teacher came in the class they started teaching in their local
language. Then seema requested them to teach in either hindi or english.

Seema seema was very alone there because all students were having freinds, or they
had took admission with their school freinds.

Teachers of that college was cooperative so they started teaching in hindi and now
she was having her full focus in studies because she wanted to do something in future.

And in the same way she completed her graduation without having any freind. But the
unwanted course and college became little bit intresting and memorable for her.
